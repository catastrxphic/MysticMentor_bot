# 🧙 MysticMentor – Your Gamified Quest Guide
MysticMentor is a Discord bot designed to turn productivity into an RPG-style adventure! Whether you're tracking tasks, earning XP, leveling up, or unlocking achievements, MysticMentor helps you stay motivated and engaged in your personal growth journey.


## ✨ Features
📜 Quest System – Complete tasks, earn XP, and progress through levels. <br />
🏆 Achievements & Badges – Unlock rewards for milestones. <br />
💰 Currency System – Earn coins for completing challenges. <br />
🎭 Role-Based Progression – Advance through ranks based on your activity. <br />
📊 Drizzle ORM + Bun.js – Fast, efficient backend with a structured database. <br />

## 🚀 Tech Stack
Backend: Bun.js, Drizzle ORM <br />
Database: libSQL <br />
Bot Framework: Discord.js
